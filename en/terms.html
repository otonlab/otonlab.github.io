Terms of Use

Made: October 2026

The Japanese version of these terms is the official one. This English version is a translation to help you understand it. If the two versions say different things, the Japanese version is the one that counts.

These terms are the rules for using the website and the apps (SOLARIS and others; we call them "the apps" below) made public by OTON LAB ("we" or "us"). When you use the apps, you agree to these terms.

1. Free to use

Anyone can use the apps for free. You do not need to sign up.

2. Please do not share copies or post them elsewhere

"Free to use" does not mean you may freely share the apps' program (source code), pictures, science data, 3D models, sounds or text. Please do not:

  • copy the apps or any part of them to give away, sell or post somewhere else
  • show the apps' screens or materials to others as if you made them
  • use the apps in a way that puts too much load on their servers (for example, downloading lots of data automatically)

The pictures, data, 3D models, fonts and software libraries made by others belong to their owners. Their own rules apply to them. Please see Copyright & Credits for details.

3. About space agencies and other organizations

The apps use data and pictures made public by organizations such as NASA (the U.S. space agency), JPL (the Jet Propulsion Laboratory) and ESA (the European Space Agency). However, we and the apps are not partners of these organizations, and they do not sponsor, approve or recommend us.

4. How correct the content is

We build the apps from published science data and try to make them as correct as we can. But they are made for learning and fun, and we cannot promise that everything is correct or complete. Please do not use them for anything where safety or important decisions depend on them, such as navigation, planning observations or disaster safety.

5. Data saved on your device, and visitor counting

Some apps save data in the web browser on your device, to show things faster or to remember your settings. You can delete this data in your browser's settings.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Access records are handled under GitHub's policies.

Counting visitors

To make the website and the apps better, we use a service called GoatCounter to count which pages are viewed, roughly where visitors come from, screen sizes and similar things. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not track you for ads. Only the person who runs this site looks at the numbers, and we do not use them to find out who you are.

6. Changes and stopping

We may change, pause or end the apps or these terms without telling you first. We are not responsible for any loss caused by this.

7. Links to other websites

The apps and the credits link to other websites. Please follow the rules of each of those websites.
