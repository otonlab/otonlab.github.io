MAIN PROJECT

SOLARIS

The universe we know,
in your hands.

A free 3D space simulator made from open science data
from NASA and others.
Visit the planets, land on the Moon,
and travel through space with real spacecraft.
You don't need to know about space.
All you need is curiosity.

Try SOLARIS →
  • The Solar System in its real positions The Sun, the 8 planets and their main moons move just as NASA JPL's data says.
  • The ISS, flying right now See where the International Space Station is now, worked out from its orbit data.
  • Today's clouds on Earth Earth's clouds are drawn from the latest weather satellite pictures.
  • Right on your phone Nothing to install and no sign-up. Just open it in your web browser.
  • In English The screens are in English. The space tours can also have English narration (an extra download).
